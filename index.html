How do I install pybullet? I keep getting “externally-managed-environment”.
Cursiv
That error means Ubuntu protects its own Python. Use a virtual environment:
  1. python3 -m venv ~/robot (once)
  2. source ~/robot/bin/activate (every new terminal)
  3. pip install pybullet — no sudo

What it does

One assistant for the whole family.

Everything lives in one plain window. Just talk to it.

Remembers each person

Cursiv learns lasting things about whoever is talking — separately for each family member. See, add or forget memories any time in “What I remember”.

With a local AI model installed, Cursiv answers on your own computer. The Setup window downloads one for you with a progress bar — no command line.

Get started

Up and running in a few minutes.

  1. Download and installRun Cursiv-Setup. Windows may say it's from an unknown publisher — click “More info” → “Run anyway”.
  2. Follow the one-screen SetupIt installs Ollama (the local AI engine) and lets you pick a model sized for your computer, with progress bars. Optional, but it's what makes Cursiv work offline.
  3. Add a free key (recommended)Paste a free Groq or Gemini key in ⚙ Settings for fast answers while you're online.

Runs on what you have

Offline speed depends on your graphics card.

With a free key, any PC is fast. For offline use, Cursiv checks your graphics card's memory (NVIDIA or AMD) and picks models that fit.

Graphics memoryRecommended modelsWhat to expect offline
No GPU / under 4 GBqwen2.5 1.5BWorks, but slower. A free key is the better everyday choice.
4–6 GBqwen2.5 3B · qwen2.5-coder 3BQuick everyday answers and solid coding help.
8 GBllama3.1 8B · qwen2.5-coder 7BFast, high-quality answers.
12–16 GB+llama3.1 · qwen2.5-coder 14BThe strongest offline coding, plus a second model that reviews the code.

Also needed: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), and 3–15 GB of free disk space depending on the models you choose. Internet is needed once, for the downloads.

Privacy, plainly

Yours by default. Online only when you choose.

No ads, no tracking, no selling anything. Here's exactly what stays put and what can go online.

🏠

Stays on your computer

  • Your conversations, memories and letters — ordinary files on your PC.
  • Your login and your keys — saved locally, never sent to us.
  • Answers from a local model — computed on your own hardware.
  • No telemetry — nothing about how you use Cursiv is collected.
🌐

Goes online only if you use it

  • Free or paid AI keys — your message goes to that provider (Groq, Google, etc.) to be answered.
  • Cursiv Cloud — a backup AI on this site, used only when no local model is ready yet. Turn it off in ⚙ Settings.
  • The phone app — its conversation, photos, and (if you allow it) a short memory summary are kept in your private space on this site.
  • Updates and problem reports — Cursiv checks GitHub for new versions; a problem report is sent only when you click Send.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Cursiv really free?

Yes. It's free and open source (MIT license). Free AI keys from Groq and Google are optional and cost nothing; paid keys are only if you want them.

Do I need to be online?

Only for the first download. After that, with a local model installed, Cursiv answers offline. When you are online, a free key makes it much faster.

Windows warned me about the installer. Is it safe?

The installer isn't code-signed yet (signing costs money), so Windows shows “unknown publisher”. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. The full source code is public on GitHub.

How does it know who's talking?

Each family member signs in with their own account on the computer, and Cursiv keeps separate memories for each person. Anyone can see or delete what it remembers about them.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. The desktop app is Windows-only today. The phone app works on any phone.

Is the AI always right?

No. Like any AI, it can be confidently wrong. Treat answers as a strong starting point and check anything that matters, especially health, money and legal questions.
